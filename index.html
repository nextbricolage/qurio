Qurio

Questions & Answers for Atlassian Confluence Cloud.

Qurio brings a fast, reliable questions-and-answers experience to Confluence: full-text search across questions and answers, embeddable page macros, voting and accepted answers, moderation, tags, notifications, and English/German localization. It is built entirely on Atlassian Forge, so your content never leaves your Atlassian instance.

Key principles

  • Runs on Atlassian Forge — hosted on Atlassian infrastructure.
  • Your data is stored in Forge Storage within your own instance.
  • No external backend, no third-party servers operated by us.
  • No AI processing, no advertising, no selling of data.
