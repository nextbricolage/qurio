Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Qurio — Q&A for Confluence ("Qurio", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when it is installed and used in an Atlassian Confluence Cloud instance. Qurio is published by nxb labs.

1. Where Qurio runs and stores data

Qurio is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs entirely on Atlassian-operated infrastructure and stores all of its data in Forge Storage, which is hosted by Atlassian and resides within your Atlassian Cloud environment. We do not operate any external server, database, or backend of our own, and your content is never transmitted to nxb labs or to any third party outside Atlassian's platform.

2. What data Qurio processes

Qurio only processes data that users create or that Atlassian provides to the app in order to render its features:

Data Purpose
Questions, answers, comments, tags, votes and "accepted answer" status Core Q&A functionality that users explicitly enter.
Atlassian accountId and public display name of the acting user To attribute questions/answers, compute statistics and expert rankings, and manage moderators and permissions.
Space key and page ID references To scope content to a space and link questions back to their source page.
Group membership (via Confluence API, read only) To enforce "who can ask / answer" permission settings when configured.
In-app notifications To notify a question's author or followers about new or accepted answers.
Per-macro and instance configuration (e.g. heading, scope, display mode) To remember how each embedded macro and the instance is configured.

Qurio does not collect email addresses, IP addresses, passwords, payment information, or any special categories of personal data. It does not use cookies or third-party analytics, and it does not perform any AI/LLM processing of your content.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to provide the app's functionality inside your Confluence instance. We do not sell, rent, share, or use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the app.

4. Access by nxb labs

Because all data stays in Forge Storage inside your instance, nxb labs has no routine access to your content. Forge lets app developers read application logs for troubleshooting; we only review logs if you contact us with a support request, and we ask you not to include sensitive information in support correspondence.

5. Data retention and deletion

Content remains in Forge Storage until it is deleted through the app or until the app is uninstalled. When you uninstall Qurio, Atlassian removes the app's Forge Storage data according to the Forge platform lifecycle. You can also delete individual questions and answers from within the app.

6. Sub-processors

Qurio's only infrastructure provider is Atlassian, which hosts the Forge runtime and storage. See Atlassian's Privacy Policy and Trust Center for details about their data handling.

7. Your rights

Because your Confluence administrator controls the instance and its data, requests to access, correct, or delete personal data are handled through your organization's Atlassian administration. We will assist controllers with such requests on reasonable notice via the contact below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
